Questions

Common questions

Is there a catch with "nothing due until you approve it"?

No catch. I build your website first and show it to you finished. If you love it, you pay and it goes live. If you don't, you don't pay.

What if I want changes?

Just tell me what you'd like changed before you approve it. After your site is live, small changes are included in the Website Care Plan, or I can quote them one at a time.

Do I need to know anything technical?

Not at all. You tell me about your business in your own words. I handle the tech and explain everything in plain language.

Can my customers order or pay online?

Yes. If you already use an ordering or payment service like Square, Toast or Clover, I link your website straight to it, so you keep managing everything there. If you don't have one yet, I'll point you to a good one.

Do you use AI?

Yes. I use AI tools to build faster, which helps keep prices low. I personally check and test everything before you see it, and you always deal with me.

Do you only work with businesses near me?

I work with people anywhere. Everything is done online, by email, text or a quick video call.
